## Important editorial gate
A language is not the same as a country. German, French, English, Arabic etc. can represent users in several jurisdictions. Country-specific legalisation claims must therefore be attached to a known document-origin country or a dedicated country page, not inferred from locale alone.

## Search-quality rule
Do not claim a locale is native-editorially complete until its 54 service titles, descriptions, body copy, FAQs and keyword set have been reviewed in that language. Programmatic presence is not editorial verification.
